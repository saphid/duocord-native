# Duocord

Duocord is an unofficial, third-party client for Discord, built natively for iPhone and iPad and designed first for iPhone Duo. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc.

- Servers, DMs, threads, forums, search, pins and an inbox in a fast native app.
- On iPhone Duo, the channel list ends at the fold and the conversation fills the other page.
- Audio in server voice channels, with a Live Activity.
- No developer servers, analytics or ads. Your session stays in your device's Keychain and is sent only to Discord.

Discord's Terms of Service don't allow third-party clients that use your account's token. Read the notice in the app before you sign in.
